## My Approach

I believe good facilities management should be:

**Reliable**

Keep critical systems operating safely and consistently.

**Efficient**

Reduce unnecessary energy, water, maintenance and operational costs.

**Measurable**

Use data to understand whether an improvement actually works.

**Sustainable**

Consider environmental impact alongside operational and financial requirements.

**Digital**

Use technology where it genuinely improves the way people work.

**Practical**

Solutions must work in the real operating environment, not only on paper.
